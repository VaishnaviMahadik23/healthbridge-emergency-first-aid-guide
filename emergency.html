Be prepared

Emergency information

Know how to recognize when immediate professional support is needed, and keep essential details ready.

If there may be an immediate danger to life

Contact your local emergency services now. Provide your location, explain what happened, and follow the dispatcher’s instructions.

When to seek immediate professional help

  • Difficulty breathing, choking, or blue/grey lips
  • Unconsciousness, seizure, confusion, or sudden collapse
  • Severe bleeding, serious injury, burns, or suspected poisoning
  • Chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, or severe allergic reaction

What to have ready

  • Your exact location and a callback number
  • A brief description of what happened and current symptoms
  • Relevant medicines, allergies, and medical information if known
  • Details of any substance or product involved

Make a plan

Emergency preparedness at home

Keep contacts, supplies, and important information accessible. Review your plan with people you live with.
